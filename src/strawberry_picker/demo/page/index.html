      How to read a report

      Ripe
      Solid outline. Ready to pick and ship.
      Unripe
      Dashed outline. Not ripe yet.
      Overripe
      Double outline. Past peak; sell fast or process.
      Reject
      Mold or rot seen. Pull the berry.
      Premium
      No defect seen. Counts toward pack-out.
